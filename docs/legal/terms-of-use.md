## 1. What this is

Rebel Match helps members of the Corporate Rebels network find peers who face
the same organizational challenge, or who have already solved it. It is run by
Transformation Architects GmbH, c/o Impact Hub Zürich AG, Sihlquai 131, 8005
Zürich, Switzerland ("we").

Rebel Match is a free beta. By using it you accept these terms. How we handle
your data is described in the privacy notice.

## 2. Your account

- Rebel Match is by invitation. An account is for one person and is not to be
  shared or passed on.
- You sign in with a link sent to your email address. Anyone who can read that
  inbox can sign in as you, so keep it safe.
- Use your real name, so that other members know who they are talking to.
- You must be at least 18 years old.

## 3. What you post

- Your challenges and notes stay yours. You allow us to store them and show
  them to other members of Rebel Match so that the app can do its job. This
  permission ends when you delete the content or your account.
- You are responsible for what you post. Do not post anything you are not
  allowed to share, such as your employer's confidential information or
  personal data about other people.
- We may show figures that combine many members' activity, such as how often a
  challenge was viewed.

## 4. How to behave

Rebel Match works on trust between peers. You agree not to:

- harass, insult or discriminate against other members,
- use the app to advertise, sell or recruit,
- use a contact detail you received through a connection for anything other
  than the exchange it was shared for, or pass it on,
- copy other members' challenges or profiles out of the app, by hand or by
  machine,
- try to get at data you are not meant to see, or disturb the running of the
  app.

## 5. What hosts may do

Hosts admit members and look after the app. They may remove content or suspend
or close an account that breaks these terms, or where it is needed to protect
other members. Where they reasonably can, they tell you why.

## 6. Leaving

You can delete your account at any time under Profile & privacy, in the menu,
or by writing to ready@transformation-architects.ch. The privacy notice says
what happens to your data then.
