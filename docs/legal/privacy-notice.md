# Rebel Match — Privacy notice

- **Status:** Approved by the maintainer on 2026-10-07; best effort, not
  reviewed by a lawyer
- **Version:** 2026-10-07
- **Shown at:** `/privacy` (R-ONB-9)

Written on a best-effort basis from the DSAT.ch model privacy notice, cut down
to what Rebel Match does. The text below the line is what the screen shows.

---

## 1. Who is responsible

Rebel Match is run by **Transformation Architects GmbH**, c/o Impact Hub Zürich
AG, Sihlquai 131, 8005 Zürich, Switzerland.

For anything about your data, write to
**ready@transformation-architects.ch**. Your contact there for data protection
is Pascal Dulex.

We have not appointed a data protection officer or a representative in the EU.

This notice covers the Rebel Match app. It follows the Swiss Data Protection
Act and, for members in the EU and EEA, the General Data Protection Regulation
(GDPR).

## 2. What we collect

Rebel Match is closed: you can only join by invitation, from a host's list or
an event invite link. We collect only what the app needs.

- **Your email address.** You sign in with it. If you ask for access without an
  invitation, we also keep the name and organization you give with the request.
- **Your profile.** Your name, and if you add them, your job title,
  organization, sector and company size.
- **What you post.** The challenges you describe, the trend each one is filed
  under, the notes you write when you offer help, and the trends you follow.
- **Your connections.** The connection requests you send and receive, and
  whether each was accepted or declined.
- **Your activity in the app.** Which challenges you were shown and how you
  responded, so that the app does not show you the same card twice and can
  tell you what happened to your requests.
- **Your choices.** The version of this notice you confirmed and when, whether
  you share usage data and since when, and how you want to be notified.
- **Messages we send you.** Sign-in links and notifications are logged with
  your email address, so that hosts can see whether a message went out.
- **Technical data.** A session cookie that keeps you signed in. Your IP
  address is used for a short time to limit abuse of the sign-in form. Error
  logs record what failed, without your name, email address or the words of
  your challenges.

We do not ask for special categories of data, such as health or political
views. Please do not put them, or personal data about other people, into a
challenge.

## 3. Why we use it, and on what grounds

- **To run the app for you**: your account, your profile, matching your
  challenge with other members, connection requests and notifications. Under
  the GDPR this rests on the agreement to provide you the service (Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **To keep the app safe and working**: limiting abuse, the log of outgoing
  messages, error logs. This rests on our legitimate interest in a secure and
  reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **To improve the app with usage data**: only if you choose to share it. This
  rests on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time
  under Profile & privacy, in the menu.
- **To meet legal duties**, should one apply to us (Art. 6(1)(c)).

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

## 4. Cookies and tracking

Rebel Match sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in. It is needed for the
app to work and is not used to follow you.

Your choice of colour mode is stored in your browser and never sent to us.

There are no advertising or analytics scripts in your browser. Fonts are served
by the app itself, so your address does not reach a font provider.

**Usage data is optional.** If you agree, our server records which screens and
buttons you use, for example that you submitted a challenge or swiped a card.
These records go to Mixpanel under a random code instead of your name. They
never contain your name, email address, organization or the words of your
challenges. If you do not agree, nothing is sent to Mixpanel about you.

## 5. Who receives your data, and where it is

- **Other members.** Your name, organization and other profile information,
  and the challenges you post, may be seen by other members of Rebel Match.
  **Your email address is shared with another member only when both of you
  accept a connection.** Until then nobody sees it, and a declined request
  reveals nothing.
- **Hosts.** The people who run Rebel Match for the network can see members,
  access requests and the log of outgoing messages, in order to admit people
  and help them. Hosts are the founders of Rebel Match and a small number of
  other people they have carefully selected.
- **Service providers** who work on our instructions:
  - Google Cloud hosts the app and its database in Zurich, Switzerland.
  - Our emails are sent from our own mail server.
  - Mixpanel stores usage data in the EU, only for members who share it.

Your data is kept in Switzerland and, for usage data, in the EU. Members can be
anywhere in the world, so what other members may see of you can be viewed from
other countries. Some providers belong to groups based in the United States;
where their staff could access data from a country without adequate protection,
this is covered by the standard contractual clauses in our agreements with them.

We pass data to authorities only where the law obliges us to.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Your account, profile, challenges, connections and activity**: for as long
  as your account exists.
- **Your activity history**: you can delete it at any time under Profile &
  privacy.
- **When you delete your account**, or ask a host to: it is deactivated at once,
  so nobody sees anything of yours, and erased after 30 days. Until then you can
  undo the deletion with the link we email you.
- **The log of outgoing messages**: 30 days.
- **Notifications**: 90 days.
- **Usage data at Mixpanel**: Mixpanel deletes event data after 24 months.
- **Backups**: copies of the database made for recovery can still contain
  deleted data for up to 90 days. They are used only to restore the app after
  a failure.

## 7. Security

Connections to the app are encrypted. You sign in with a link sent to your
email address, so there is no password to lose. Access to members' data is
limited to the hosts who need it, and the database cannot be reached from the
internet.

No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong that puts your data at
risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 8. Do you have to give us data?

You need an email address and a name to use Rebel Match. Everything else in
your profile is optional, and so is sharing usage data. Without the email
address and name we cannot offer you the app.

## 9. Automated processing

The app files each challenge under one of eight trends by looking for keywords
in its text, and you can correct the result. It suggests members to each other
on the basis of those trends. No decision with legal or similarly significant
effect on you is made automatically.

## 10. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy,
- correct data that is wrong,
- delete your data,
- restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it,
- hand over the data you gave us in a common electronic format.

You can withdraw your consent to usage data at any time. Doing so does not
affect what was recorded before.

Much of this you can do yourself under Profile & privacy: edit your profile,
delete your activity history, change the usage data choice and delete your
account. For anything else, write to ready@transformation-architects.ch. We
answer within 30 days and may ask you to show that the account is yours.

You may also complain to a data protection authority. In Switzerland this is
the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (www.edoeb.admin.ch).
In the EU or EEA it is the authority of the country where you live or work.

## 11. Changes to this notice

We may change this notice when the app or the law changes. The current version
and its date are shown at the top. When a change affects how we use your data,
the app shows you the new summary and asks you to confirm it before you
continue.
